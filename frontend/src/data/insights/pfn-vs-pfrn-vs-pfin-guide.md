PFN, PFRN and PFIN get used interchangeably in purchase orders across India — and that causes wrong deliveries, delayed surgeries and billing disputes. They are **three distinct nail systems**. This guide explains the difference in plain terms, from a distributor that stocks and dispatches all three across Telangana every week.

## The one-paragraph answer

**PFN (Proximal Femoral Nail)** is the classic intertrochanteric fracture nail using two proximal screws (a lag screw plus an anti-rotation hip pin). **PFRN (Proximal Femoral Rotation-control Nail)** is the newer generation using a helical blade or enhanced screw design for better rotational stability in osteoporotic bone. **PFIN (Proximal Femoral Integrated Nail)** integrates the proximal fixation into a combined mechanism for specific fracture patterns. If your surgeon says "PFN2" or "nail with blade", they usually mean a PFRN-class implant.

## When each system is chosen

| System | Typical indication | Bone quality | Proximal fixation |
|---|---|---|---|
| PFN | Stable intertrochanteric fractures | Good bone stock | Lag screw + derotation screw |
| PFRN | Unstable/comminuted IT fractures | Osteoporotic bone | Helical blade / rotation-control screw |
| PFIN | Selected patterns needing integrated fixation | Mixed | Integrated mechanism |

## Sizing that purchase teams get wrong

- **Diameter:** 9 to 12 mm are the workhorse sizes. Confirm from the pre-op templating, not from "whatever came last time."
- **Length:** short nails (180–240 mm) for standard IT fractures; **long nails (300–420 mm)** for subtrochanteric extension or pathological fractures. A "PFRN nail Ø11 L300" is a long-nail case — make sure the long-nail set is booked, not the short set.
- **Neck angle:** 130° and 135° are both stocked; the surgeon's templating decides.
- **Side matters on some systems:** long nails have left/right bow — a TAN LEFT nail cannot go into a right femur.

## Titanium vs stainless steel

Titanium nails dominate current practice: better biocompatibility, MRI-friendlier, and the standard for premium sets (Meril's titanium PFRN systems are what we dispatch most). Stainless remains available where budget is the deciding factor.

## What a complete set dispatch includes

A proper proximal femoral nailing dispatch is not just the nail. Confirm the box contains: the nail (correct diameter, length, angle, side), the proximal screws or helical blades in a size run, distal locking bolts, end caps, and the **instrumentation tray** for that specific system — PFN instruments do not fit PFRN implants. Sets travel with a batch-tracked challan and return within the agreed loaner window.
