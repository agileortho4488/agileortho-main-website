## Destiknee vs Freedom Knee: A Technical Comparison in Total Knee Arthroplasty

Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) remains one of the most successful orthopedic interventions for alleviating pain and restoring function in patients suffering from end-stage knee osteoarthritis or other debilitating knee pathologies. The choice of implant system is a critical determinant of long-term success, influencing kinematics, wear characteristics, and patient satisfaction. This article provides a highly technical and clinical comparison between two prominent TKA systems: the Destiknee™ and the FREEDOM Total Knee System, leveraging precise product data from Agile Healthcare to delineate their distinct features, potential clinical outcomes, and target patient demographics.

### Destiknee™ Total Knee Replacement System: Engineering for High Flexion and Asian Demographics

The Destiknee™ system is engineered with a focus on achieving high flexion and optimal fit, particularly for Asian patient populations.

#### Design and Materials
The Destiknee™ Fixed Bearing Hinge Knee features a **7-Radii Progressive multi-radii design**. This sophisticated articulation aims to mimic natural knee kinematics throughout the range of motion, promoting stability and reducing patellofemoral stress. The system boasts an impressive **flexion range of up to 155°**, a critical consideration for patients with cultural or activity-based requirements for deep knee bending.

In terms of materials, the femoral and tibial components are constructed from **Cobalt Chrome**, a robust alloy known for its excellent wear resistance and biocompatibility. The articular surface, specifically the liner, is made from **UHMWPE (Ultra-High Molecular Weight Polyethylene)**, a standard in orthopedic implants for its low friction and durability. The system incorporates a **5-point tibial locking system** to ensure secure fixation and stability of the tibial insert. Furthermore, the Destiknee™ Total Knee Replacement specifies a **surface roughness of 11 µm**, a parameter optimized to minimize wear and enhance implant longevity.
