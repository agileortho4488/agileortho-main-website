Cath labs stocking drug-eluting stents (DES) usually end up carrying more than one platform — different drug coatings suit different lesion types and physician preference. Here's how our two most-stocked DES platforms actually differ.

## The core difference: the drug

- **BioMime** is a **sirolimus-eluting** stent on a cobalt-chromium platform with a biodegradable polymer coating.
- **Evermine50** is an **everolimus-eluting** stent — everolimus is a sirolimus analog with somewhat faster tissue uptake and a slightly different anti-restenotic profile.

Neither drug has a settled clinical edge across all lesion types in the literature — the choice is usually made on physician preference, deliverability, and sizing availability for the specific lesion, not drug pharmacology alone.

## Why cath labs stock both

1. **Sizing gaps** — no single platform covers every diameter/length combination a busy cath lab needs; carrying two platforms closes gaps.
2. **Physician preference** — interventional cardiologists often have a platform preference from training or personal case experience; stocking both avoids case-day substitution.
3. **Supply continuity** — a single-platform lab is exposed if that specific SKU goes on backorder; a second platform is a practical hedge, not just a clinical choice.
